Connect with a friend

This static GitHub Pages version uses a manual connection code. No server or account is needed.

  1. Both open this page in Chrome or Edge.
  2. One person clicks Create offer and sends the code.
  3. The other pastes it and clicks Create answer.
  4. Send the answer back; the first person pastes it and clicks Connect.
Not connected yet.

Tip: the person who creates the offer is the one who shares the browser tab. Share the tab before creating the offer. Choose “Chrome Tab” and enable “Share tab audio” in the browser picker. Your browser may also ask permission for your microphone.

How website sharing works

The host shares a browser tab. Your friend receives a live video and audio stream. This works with many sites, but protected services may show a black screen or block capture because of DRM or browser restrictions.

MovieTogether is a peer-to-peer demo. Connection codes are temporary. Never share them publicly.
